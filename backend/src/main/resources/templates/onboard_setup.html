Step 2 · Subjects

Every ACADIA school starts with 5 default subjects (Math, Science, Social Science, English, Language) already active — this step is optional. You can add, rename or deactivate them here, or later from Subjects in the sidebar.

Changes save instantly. Collapse this and continue when you're done.

Add teachers and additional admins to your school. Each one can sign in straight away with the temporary password shown here — inviting them from this console is the approval.
