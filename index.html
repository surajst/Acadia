Your school does great work every day. Parents see almost none of it.

Updates are buried in WhatsApp groups. Attendance lives in registers, marks in Excel. Parents only hear from school when something goes wrong — and that is the story they tell other parents.

For parents

“What did my child do today?” Answered.

Attendance, homework, results and teacher feedback land on the parent’s phone as they happen — separately for each child. No group-chat noise. No chasing the class teacher.

And parents don’t just watch. They reward effort at home with Parent points their child sees at school.
