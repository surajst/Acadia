One platform · three homes

Every person at school gets a home built for them.

Students, parents and teachers each open to their own screen — the same calm design, with exactly what that person needs today. Everything else is one tap away on the wheel.

School that feels like levelling up.

A home screen that answers “how am I doing?” — Scholar Level, XP from school and from home, today's quest and the next class.

    The wheel

    Everything a person needs, one tap from home.

    No buried menus. Each role's destinations sit on a single wheel around them — tap a spoke to see what's behind it.

    Student · Timetable

    Today's periods, at a glance

    The day's classes, rooms and times — with the next one always surfaced on the home screen.

    Rewards that connect home and school

    Two ledgers. One cheering section.

    Teachers award School XP — it drives the Scholar Level. Parents award Parent XP for effort at home — its own ledger, spent on rewards the family sets. A child sees both, side by side, every day.
