Privacy policy

ACADIA app and portal · Last updated 5 November 2026

ACADIA is a school platform made by Concept Edu, Bengaluru, India. Schools use it to share timetables, homework, attendance, marks, fees and messages with students, parents and teachers. This policy explains what the ACADIA mobile app (Android, and the web app at app.concept-edu.com) and the staff portal (portal.concept-edu.com) collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible for your data

Your account is created by your school, and your school decides what information is kept about its students, parents and staff. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, your school is the data fiduciary, and Concept Edu processes the data on the school's behalf to run ACADIA. Where the app is used by a child, the school is responsible for obtaining a parent's or guardian's consent. Questions about the records your school keeps are best sent to your school first; you can also reach us using the form on our account requests page.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, role (student, parent, teacher, driver, admin), school, class and section, and the link between a parent and their children.
  • School records that your school enters or that you submit: timetables, homework and submissions, attendance, marks and report cards, syllabus progress, rewards points, fee records, announcements and feedback.
  • Messages you send in the app, including voice messages if you choose to record one (the app asks for microphone access only when you start recording).
  • Photos: a profile photo if you add one (from your camera or gallery, only when you choose to), and photos your school shares in its gallery.
  • Bus location: only for school bus drivers, and only while the driver has started a trip and keeps the app open. Parents see the bus's live position during the trip. ACADIA does not collect the location of students, parents or teachers, and does not track location in the background.
  • Technical data: the app version and basic device information needed to deliver app updates, and error reports from our servers that do not include your personal data.

ACADIA has no advertising and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. We do not sell personal data or share it for marketing.

How we use it

  • To run the app for your school: show each person the information their role allows, send notifications about homework, attendance, results, fees and announcements, and let parents follow their own child's progress.
  • To keep the service secure: sign-in, access control between schools and roles, and an audit log of changes.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

Who can see it

Information is visible only inside your school, according to role: for example, parents see their own children's records, and teachers see the classes they teach. Data from one school is never visible to another school. We use these service providers to run ACADIA, and they process data only on our instructions:

  • Render (application hosting) and Neon (database), with data stored in Singapore
  • Expo (delivery of app updates)
  • Google Maps (showing the bus location on a map)
  • Sentry (server error monitoring, configured not to collect personal data)

We may disclose information if Indian law requires it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as hashes, and access is checked on every request against your school and role.

How long we keep it

We keep data for as long as your school uses ACADIA and your account is active. When an account is deleted, it is deactivated immediately and the remaining personal data is deleted within 30 days, except records your school is required to keep by law (such as marks and fee receipts), which stay with the school. Deleted data is removed from backups as those backups expire.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. Or, without the app, use our account deletion page.
  • Remove your profile photo at any time from your profile.
  • Turn off location, camera or microphone access in your phone's settings. Only the related feature stops working.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for a correction, through your school or our account requests page.

Children

Students, including children under 13, use ACADIA only through an account their school creates. We do not show ads to anyone, and we use children's data only to provide the school service.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above, and tell schools about significant changes.
