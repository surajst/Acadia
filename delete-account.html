Delete your ACADIA account

For students, parents, teachers and drivers who use the ACADIA app

In the app

Open ACADIA, go to Settings, and tap Delete account. Your account is deactivated straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Send us this form. We confirm your identity with your school and then delete the account.

What happens

  • Your account is deactivated immediately and can no longer sign in.
  • Your profile photo is deleted immediately.
  • Your other personal data (name, email, messages, voice messages) is deleted within 30 days.
  • Records your school must keep by law, such as marks, attendance registers and fee receipts, stay with the school.

Deleting a parent account does not delete the child's student account. To delete a student account, ask the school or send this form as the parent.

Thanks. We've received your request and will confirm by email once it's done. That's usually within a few days, and always within 30 days.
